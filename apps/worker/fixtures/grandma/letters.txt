My dearest sweetheart,

Remember the lake house? The water was so cold in June it would steal your breath, but you'd jump in anyway and laugh that big laugh of yours. Your grandfather would shake his head and say "that one's got fire in her bones."

I made the lemon cake again on Sunday — yours always rose better than mine. I think it's because you talk to it. You always said the cake knows when you're paying attention.

Tell your mother I said to wear the green coat in the cold. She'll listen if it comes from me.

All my love, always,
Grandma

Birthday card, 2014:

Eighteen years. Where did they go? I blinked and you grew tall.

Promise me three things: be kind to your sister even when she's being silly, write things down so you don't forget the small days, and call me on Sundays. The Sundays are long now.

I love you to the moon and back, kiddo.

— G
